🎨 PAN Card Editor

Edit and correct your PAN card details

📋 How to Use:

  • Enter the text you want to add in the "Text to Add" field
  • Customize font size, family, color, and weight as needed
  • Click "Add Text" button, then click anywhere on the PAN card to place your text
  • Adjust background opacity to see your edits more clearly
  • Use "Undo Last" to remove the most recent text
  • Click "Download Edited PAN" to save your corrected card
  • Use "Reset All" to start over with the original image
